Annotation guide

For each sample you see the image, the original text, and the atomic statements taken from that text.

  • Rate every statement from 0 to 4 for how strongly the image supports it, or choose Cannot judge.
  • Then give one overall 1–7 judgement of how well the complete image and text match. It appears once every statement is rated.

Cannot judge means the image genuinely does not give you enough evidence — severe occlusion, something outside the frame. It is not a way to skip a hard item.

Skip for now postpones a sample so you can return to it later. It stays in your assignment.

If something is broken — the image will not load, the text is garbled — use Report a technical issue. That is different from both of the above.

Details
  • 0 — Not supported / contradicted. The image clearly does not support the statement, or shows something incompatible with it.
  • 1 — Weakly supported. Little supporting evidence; the image is mostly inconsistent with the statement.
  • 2 — Partially supported. Some aspects are supported, but an important part is missing, unclear or inconsistent.
  • 3 — Strongly supported. Mostly supported, with only minor uncertainty or incompleteness.
  • 4 — Fully supported. Clearly and completely supported by the image.
  • Cannot judge. The image genuinely does not provide enough evidence to decide.
