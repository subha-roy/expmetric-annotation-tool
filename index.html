Annotation guide

Each sample has three steps. Please do them in order — the order is part of the study design.

  • Step 1 — Decomposition (text only). You see a text and a list of atomic statements taken from it. Judge each statement against the text alone. The image is deliberately hidden here.
  • Step 2 — Overall alignment. The image is revealed. Give one overall 1–7 judgement of how well the image and text match, before looking at the statements again.
  • Step 3 — Statement support. For each statement, rate how well the image supports it (0–4), or choose Cannot judge.

Cannot judge means the image genuinely does not let you decide. It is not a way to skip a hard item. If something is technically broken (image will not load, text is garbled), use Report a technical issue instead — that is different.
