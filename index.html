Annotation guide

Each sample has three steps. Please complete them in order; the order is part of the study design.

  • Step 1 — Decomposition (text only). You see the text and atomic statements extracted from it. Judge whether each statement is a reasonable decomposition of the text. The image is deliberately hidden.
  • Step 2 — Statement support. The image is revealed. For each atomic statement, rate how strongly the image supports it from 0 to 4, or choose Cannot judge.
  • Step 3 — Overall alignment. After rating all statements, give one overall 1–7 judgement of how well the complete image and text match.

Cannot judge means the image genuinely does not provide enough evidence to decide. It is not a way to skip a difficult item.

If something is technically broken, use Report a technical issue instead.

What each score means
  • 0 — Not supported / contradicted. The image clearly does not support the statement, or clearly shows the opposite.
  • 1 — Weakly supported. Very little supporting visual evidence; the image is mostly inconsistent with the statement.
  • 2 — Partially supported. Some of the statement is supported, but important visual information is missing, unclear or inconsistent.
  • 3 — Strongly supported. Mostly supported, with only minor uncertainty or incompleteness.
  • 4 — Fully supported. Clearly and completely supported by the image.
  • Cannot judge. The image genuinely does not provide enough evidence — severe occlusion, the entity is outside the frame, or the view is insufficient. Not a way to skip a difficult item.
